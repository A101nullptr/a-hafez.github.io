CenterOfGravity

The real centre of an element, from its actual geometry

Platform | Revit
Stack | C# / .NET · WinForms · Revit API · solid tessellation

Lifting and transport planning needs an element's true centre of gravity, and a bounding-box centre gets that wrong for any irregular shape. This add-in tessellates the solid to calculate the volume-weighted centroid of each selected element, then places a marker there. A modeless window shows the ID, family, volume and X, Y, Z in project units, and steps through several selected elements. On close, it asks whether to delete the markers.

Why the geometry matters

Design decisions

Real solidsTessellating the geometry gives the true centroid for irregular elements.
Modeless windowYou keep working in Revit, and the values follow your selection.
Marker with dataThe coordinates are written into the marker family, so they stay in the model.
Leaves no messClear removes all markers, and closing offers to as well.
