WallChecker

Finds the walls that are almost right

Platform | Revit
Stack | C# / .NET · WPF · Revit API

A wall that's a fraction of a millimetre off grid causes problems later, in joins, dimensions and prefabrication data, and it's almost invisible in a drawing. WallChecker audits every wall's length, end-point coordinates and angle, which must each be an exact multiple of a chosen step. Failing walls are listed, and one click zooms straight to each.

Why a wall gets flagged

Three checks

LengthA multiple of 0.5 mm, 0.25 mm or a custom value.
PositionStart and end coordinates, against the same tolerance.
OrientationA multiple of 90°, 45° or a custom angle, with an optional offset.
Element listFailing walls can be selected and fixed directly while the window stays open.
